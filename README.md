# Week 12 – HR Business Analysis Dashboard

## Objective

The objective of this project is to analyze employee-related data using Tableau Public and identify meaningful business insights, patterns, comparisons, and relationships.

## Tools Used

- Tableau Public
- Microsoft Excel / CSV Dataset
- GitHub

## Dashboard

### Visualizations

1. **Age Distribution – Histogram**
   - Shows the distribution of employees across different age groups.

2. **Employee Analysis – Circle Chart**
   - Compares employee-related information across departments and business travel categories.

3. **Business Travel – Continuous Area Chart**
   - Shows the distribution of employees based on business travel categories and age.

4. **Age Analysis – Line Chart**
   - Displays employee age patterns across different education fields.

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard includes interactive filters to explore the data:

- **Department**
- **Business Travel**
- **Age**
- **Education Field**

These filters allow users to analyze specific employee groups and compare different categories.

## Key Findings

- Employee distribution varies across different age groups and departments.
- Business travel patterns differ among employees and can be compared using the dashboard filters.
- Different education fields show different employee age distributions.
- The interactive filters help identify patterns within specific departments and employee groups.

## Business Recommendations

- Organizations can analyze employee distribution by department to improve workforce planning.
- Business travel requirements can be reviewed to understand employee workload and travel patterns.
- Employee demographics and education fields can be considered when planning recruitment and workforce development strategies.

## Conclusion

The Tableau dashboard provides an interactive view of employee data and helps identify important patterns based on age, department, business travel, and education field. The dashboard can support better workforce analysis and business decision-making.
